# Genesis Pool

A self-running evolution simulation. Simple cells with tiny evolving brains graze on living algae, and over time the world changes around them: algae fill the water with oxygen, seas recede, land rises, and the occasional asteroid or supervolcano resets the board. Predators, armor, giants, bigger brains and land animals evolve on their own. You only watch.

Live: https://nathansix.github.io/genesis-pool/

- Everything runs in the browser from one file, `index.html`.
- Each browser autosaves its own world and catches up on time spent away (up to about 3 minutes of evolution at normal pace).
